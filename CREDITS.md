## Sounds

The nine sound effects in `AngryKeyboard/Sounds` come from
[Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/):

- `combat-impact.mp3`
- `explode-rock.mp3`
- `kung-fu-yell.mp3`
- `punch.mp3`
- `punch-impact-hit.mp3`
- `rocket-whoosh.mp3`
- `shotgun.mp3`
- `shotgun-blast.mp3`
- `shotgun-cocking.mp3`

The [Pixabay Content
License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) allows commercial and
non-commercial use without attribution, and it does not permit redistributing
the raw file as a standalone product. Bundling the sounds inside the app is not
that.

The MIT license covers the code only. It does not cover the sounds.
